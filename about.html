I'm Manolis. I work as a risk manager in the fintech sector, after a career that combines IT engineering with the development of financial solutions. I have also been trading for over 25 years, with crude oil at the centre: a passion that over time became an important part of my professional path and of my experience in the markets.

XenosFinance is the platform I built for my own market work and decided to open to everyone.

The website, the Elliott Wave engine, the Telegram bot and the reports are designed, coded and maintained by me. There is no editorial team and there are no investors: when something gets published, one person stands behind it.

How I work

  • Elliott Wave as the framework for reading market structure, with explicit rules and invalidation levels.
  • Crude oil at the core: weekly EIA inventories, COT positioning on WTI and Brent, futures flows.
  • Macro and geopolitics for context: central banks, data releases and the events that move energy and currencies.
  • Risk before reward: every setup comes with a stop, an invalidation level and a risk/reward ratio.

How the content is produced

Part of the analysis is automated: indicators, market scans and text drafts produced with AI tools from real market data.

Details are in the AI Disclaimer and the Risk Disclaimer.

Independence

XenosFinance carries no advertising, is not an introducing broker and takes no commissions from brokers or exchanges. I work only with small groups of students and investors. The Premium plan covers server costs.

Nothing published here is personalised financial advice. Leveraged trading carries a high risk of loss.
