Federal Reserve policymakers signal intent to raise interest rates on December 9 without waiting for the November inflation report, aiming for a 2% target by 2027. These divergent trends highlight persistent global monetary tightening alongside isolated regional growth surprises.


EQUITIES

Conversely, safe-haven consumer staples like Kroger and Tyson Foods slide as capital rotates back into high-beta growth stocks. COMMODITIES & ENERGY

Brent crude trades subdued at $88.67 per barrel and WTI slips below $80 per barrel, reaching 13-week lows as supply concerns ease. The market largely ignores reports of three oil tankers striking mines in the Strait of Hormuz alongside cyberattack threats at the Corpus Christi export port. Natural gas prices drop on mild weather forecasts and reduced heating demand, while US refiner margins shrink due to persistent gasoline oversupply. Spot gold gains $103 as investors maintain defensive hedges against macro uncertainty.


CRYPTO

Bitcoin rebounds 17% from $55,000 lows to touch $64,500, triggered by the broader tech market surge and over $500 million in short liquidations. CFTC staff issue key guidance permitting exchanges to count Bitcoin, Ether, and payment stablecoins toward official operating reserves. FOREX & RATES

The Singapore Dollar gains firm support after strong Q3 GDP numbers reduce pressure on the Monetary Authority of Singapore to ease policy in January. US Treasury yields remain elevated with two-year paper settling at 4.700% ahead of expected Fed tightening. In Japan, spot electricity prices hit their highest levels since August 2022 due to early cold weather forecasts and rising gas demand, influencing domestic inflation expectations. Major currency pairs consolidate as foreign exchange volatility stays contained within tight ranges.


GEOPOLITICAL RISK

Tensions escalate in the Middle East as Iran asserts three oil tankers struck mines in the Strait of Hormuz, threatening key transit routes. Renewed US-Israeli strategic moves toward Iran raise risks of economic disruption and geopolitical realignment across Central Asian republics. In the United States, the Port of Corpus Christi faces thousands of daily cyberattack attempts targeting vital crude export infrastructure. Additionally, COP31 climate negotiations stall without a consensus on financial commitments for developing nations.


MARKET OUTLOOK

Traders look ahead to key Asian economic events and upcoming central bank commentary to gauge interest rate trajectories. Energy traders track Strait of Hormuz shipping developments alongside changing weather forecasts impacting natural gas demand.
