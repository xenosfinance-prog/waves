Traders will closely watch upcoming inflation reports, particularly the US inflation report, which could influence central bank rate hike expectations for December. Continued monitoring of geopolitical developments in Ukraine, Libya, and the Middle East is crucial for assessing risk sentiment. Energy market dynamics, including European natural gas prices and global oil supply, will remain key drivers.
