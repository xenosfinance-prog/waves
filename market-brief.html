Conversely, optimism surrounding Brazil’s presidential election is currently providing a counter-narrative of growth opportunity in emerging markets.


MARKET OUTLOOK

Traders remain laser-focused on the upcoming U.S. CPI report, which serves as the next critical indicator for Fed policy direction. The 24-48 hour window requires monitoring potential escalation in Middle East energy transport corridors, which could trigger a sharp move in crude oil and bond yields. Key technical levels include Brent oil consolidation near $105 and London Gas Oil testing the $1,382 support level.
