Gold encounters downside pressure as sellers test key support near the $3,500 level.

FOREX & RATES

The US Dollar Index strengthens as a unwinding of risk assets boosts demand for safe-haven currencies ahead of US inflation data. Sterling slipped against the dollar as market participants de-risk positions prior to the Personal Consumption Expenditures release. The Japanese yen retains underlying support from the 4.2% jump in October services inflation, which solidifies Bank of Japan rate hike expectations. Overall FX market volatility remains elevated while global liquidity conditions continue to tighten.


GEOPOLITICAL RISK

Trade friction escalated as the US government proposed fresh tariffs on China and other trade partners following court challenges to previous levies. In the Middle East, tanker disruptions near the Strait of Hormuz accelerate a shift in global shipping, driving a record number of LR2 clean tankers into crude transportation. Iranian authorities attribute recent maritime incidents to naval mines, compounding security concerns along key transit choke points. These geopolitical developments continue to disrupt energy logistics and maintain structural inflation pressures.


MARKET OUTLOOK

Traders in the next 24 to 48 hours must monitor the incoming US PCE inflation print to confirm Federal Reserve policy expectations ahead of December. Key technical levels to watch include gold holding the $3,500 support threshold and Bitcoin defending the $53,200 level amid broader tech equity weakness. Foreign exchange traders will monitor Bank of Japan hawkish policy signals and Japanese yield moves. Any further headlines regarding US trade tariffs or Strait of Hormuz disruptions represent primary tail risks for asset markets.
