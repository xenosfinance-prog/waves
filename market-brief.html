However, potential trade friction threatens European and Asian supply chains following massive trade surplus figures out of China.

Gold trades in a volatile range near key levels as market participants await clear signals from U.S. inflation data and currency moves.

FOREX & RATES

Treasury yields remain sensitive to potential inflation surprises, with a firm core reading expected to boost the greenback against major peers. Sterling faces mixed signals after UK retail sales increase 1.5%, though higher headline spending largely reflects elevated fuel costs rather than underlying consumer strength. Volatility across major FX pairs remains muted during European trading sessions as markets await macroeconomic catalysts.


GEOPOLITICAL RISK

Tensions in the Middle East moderate as unconfirmed reports of Qatari-Pakistani mediation revive U.S.-Iran diplomatic discussions and lower risk premiums. European diplomats meet G7 foreign ministers in Paris to lobby for European inclusion in upcoming U.S.-mediated Russia-Ukraine talks scheduled in Abu Dhabi. China's trade surplus surpasses $1 trillion as October exports beat forecasts and imports surge 41.6%, heightening the probability of new tariffs from the European Union. Additionally, U.S. traders encounter operational hurdles regarding President Trump's agreement with Vladimir Putin as Russian diesel tankers divert from American ports.


MARKET OUTLOOK

Over the next 24 to 48 hours, global financial markets focus almost entirely on the outcome of the U.S. CPI print and subsequent Federal Reserve commentary. Traders must monitor whether Treasury yields spike on core inflation persistence, which would challenge high-valuation tech equities. In energy markets, crude oil requires monitoring around the $90 Brent support level as diplomatic developments unfold. Furthermore, supply chain and technology shares face headline risks tied to emerging global trade barrier announcements following China's record trade surplus.
