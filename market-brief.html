MACRO & CENTRAL BANKS

Global markets remain locked in narrow trading ranges as market participants prepare for the Federal Reserve policy decision, where expectations lean toward a hawkish hold. In Europe, equity markets move lower, with the UK 100 dropping 0.73% while global tech names experience localized buying interest.


COMMODITIES & ENERGY

Crude oil prices maintain elevated levels as Brent crude holds near $115 per barrel driven by Middle East tension and supply line risks in the Strait of Hormuz. OPEC+ key producers plan to keep existing production quotas unchanged for December to sustain market stability. On the domestic regulatory front, US policy moves aim to ease federal methane restrictions to lower energy production costs. In refined products, surging diesel demand lifts India fuel export revenue by 50% year-over-year to 2.59 trillion rupees.


CRYPTO

Bitcoin experiences short-term downside pressure, dropping below the $80,000 threshold as market participants de-risk prior to the Fed meeting. Despite near-term price softness, long-term on-chain metrics show a falling share of active supply from older coins, signaling early structural bull cycle tendencies. Institutional asset management on-chain continues to expand, highlighted by Janus Henderson launching a tokenized Nasdaq-100 fund on Stellar and Ripple expanding custody tie-ups with BNY. Furthermore, crypto miners and derivatives markets increasingly pivot toward commercializing AI compute capacity.


FOREX & RATES

The US Dollar Index trades with a slight upward bias, supported by an equity rebound in tech while the euro weakens amid ongoing domestic political uncertainties in France. The Canadian dollar edges lower as foreign exchange traders position for upcoming monetary policy announcements from both the Bank of Canada and the Fed. In digital currency infrastructure, European MiCA regulations drive USDC market share in euro stablecoin trading to 56%, displacing competing stablecoin volume.


GEOPOLITICAL RISK

Trade friction rises following warnings that doubled 50% import tariffs on Canadian vehicles and auto parts would create severe disruptions for the automotive industry. In Eastern Europe, military activity in Ukraine targets core municipal bridges as European leaders initiate direct dialogue with Moscow to explore negotiations. Additionally, expanded nuclear and technological cooperation between Russia and Iran underscores deepening bilateral alignments designed to navigate international sanctions.


MARKET OUTLOOK

Over the next 24 to 48 hours, global asset price action will anchor directly to monetary policy outcomes and press conferences from the Federal Reserve and the Bank of Canada. Traders should closely monitor the $80,000 technical level for Bitcoin alongside movements in short-term US Treasury yields for immediate directional guidance. Energy markets remain sensitive to headlines regarding OPEC+ quota confirmations and maritime supply security in the Middle East.
