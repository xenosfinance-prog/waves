Investors are cautioned to prepare for continued volatility despite the AI rally.

Equinor secured its longest gas supply deal in 40 years, while Electric and Hybrid Cars now capture three-quarters of Europe's auto market.

In the US, the Treasury successfully sold $69 billion in two-year notes at a high yield of 4.700%.


GEOPOLITICAL RISK

There are claims from Iran that three oil tankers struck mines in the Strait of Hormuz, although this has not significantly impacted oil prices to date. America's largest oil export port is also fending off thousands of daily cyberattack attempts.


MARKET OUTLOOK

Traders should monitor the upcoming German IFO data for further insights into European economic health. Continued volatility in AI-related equities, particularly Nvidia, is expected following its recent surge. Energy markets will watch for further developments regarding oil supply dynamics and geopolitical tensions impacting the Strait of Hormuz.
