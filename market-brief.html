EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Global financial markets display a mixed tone today as traders position ahead of crucial US CPI inflation data and absorb a sharp drop in energy prices. Crude oil plunges over 7 percent following signals of diplomatic restraint between the US and Iran, while Asian equities stage a rebound led by strong AI hardware demand. Macro sentiment remains cautious with Federal Reserve officials signaling potential interest rate hikes in December.


MACRO & CENTRAL BANKS

The upcoming US CPI report takes center stage, with markets focused on the monthly core reading to gauge Federal Reserve monetary policy. Fed Governor Waller notes he is leaning toward a rate increase in December and opens the door to a potential half-point hike depending on inflation data. Meanwhile, Fed Governor Goolsbee highlights stubborn service-sector inflation despite a steady labor market. Wholesale inventories rose 0.7 percent in September, beating expectations of 0.6 percent.


EQUITIES

Asian equity markets lead a global recovery as the Nikkei and KOSPI surge on renewed buying in artificial intelligence and semiconductor stocks. The stock market rally continues to broaden beyond Big Tech, though investors remain cautious ahead of upcoming inflation releases. European indices show targeted strength, with Playtech shares jumping over 3 percent following an upgraded 2026 earnings forecast. Oxford Instruments also records solid gains amid strong sector interest.


COMMODITIES & ENERGY

Crude oil prices plunge over 7 percent, with Brent falling to 91.61 dollars per barrel and WTI dropping to 86 dollars as reports indicate President Trump is exercising restraint regarding Iran. Spot gold rises nearly 2 percent before stabilizing near key support levels ahead of the US CPI data release. Energy demand signals remain robust in Asia, where China crude oil imports hit their highest level since March. Additionally, LNG traffic through the Strait of Hormuz rises to 31 tankers, marking its highest volume since February.


CRYPTO

Crypto asset prices remain under pressure, forcing major Bitcoin miners to liquidate holdings to support ongoing operations and AI infrastructure expansion. Marathon Digital sold 13,597 Bitcoin for 1.1 billion dollars over six weeks, while both MARA and HIVE report lower mining revenue despite higher production. Security firm Elliptic identifies North Korean hackers using memecoins on the NEAR protocol to launder stolen funds. On the regulatory and infrastructure front, Formation Securities announces plans for a Solana-based tokenized stock trading venue.


FOREX & RATES

The US Dollar index consolidates near recent highs as market participants await the CPI report to price in December Fed rate expectations. Sterling slips against the greenback despite UK retail sales rising 1.5 percent, as higher fuel costs rather than underlying consumer demand drive the retail figures. Foreign exchange volatility remains compressed in European morning trading ahead of key economic data. The US NFIB Small Business Optimism Index is expected to print at 98.2, up from 97.9 previously.


GEOPOLITICAL RISK

Geopolitical tension in the Middle East eases slightly after reports indicate the US is avoiding direct military escalation with Iran, unwinding significant risk premia in energy markets. China reports a record trade surplus exceeding 1 trillion dollars powered by AI exports, alongside a notable 41.6 percent surge in imports. However, China massive trade surplus increases the likelihood of renewed trade friction and tariff threats from European and Western governments. Meanwhile, US traders continue to assess logistics surrounding Russian diesel shipments under recent diplomatic agreements.


MARKET OUTLOOK

Over the next 24 to 48 hours, market direction heavily depends on the US CPI inflation reading and subsequent commentary from Federal Reserve speakers. A monthly core inflation print of 0.3 percent or higher could reignite hawkish rate expectations and push Treasury yields higher. Traders must monitor Brent crude around the 91 dollar support level for signs of stabilization following the diplomatic unwinding. Equity investors should track whether the regional AI technology rebound can sustain momentum against macro headwinds.
