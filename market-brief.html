German September CPI is expected to show inflation returning above 3%, which could impact EUR pairs.

Geopolitical developments in the Middle East continue to underpin energy prices, as Trump's stance on Iran sanctions limits supply and the war traps LNG behind the Strait of Hormuz. Trade tensions are noted as Beijing warns of retaliation against potential EU curbs on Chinese businesses, and China adds a 55% tariff to Brazil beef imports.


MARKET OUTLOOK

Traders will closely monitor the full German September CPI report today for further insights into European inflation trends and potential ECB policy implications. The ongoing Middle East situation and its impact on global energy supply will remain a critical market driver. Fresh Chinese economic data releases and any rhetoric regarding trade disputes with the EU will also be important risk events in the next 24-48 hours.
