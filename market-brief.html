MARKET OUTLOOK

Traders should monitor for sustained weakness in the equity and crypto markets, particularly in tech stocks like Nvidia and Anthropic. The tug-of-war in oil prices between growing supply and persistent geopolitical risks in the Middle East will remain a key focus. Commentary surrounding interest rates and any new developments in US-Iran relations or the Ukraine conflict could quickly shift market sentiment.
