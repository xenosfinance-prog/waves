CRYPTO

FOREX & RATES

Gold was hit hard, trading under $4200 again amidst broader market volatility. The Japanese yen jumped following comments from Japan's top currency diplomat Mimura, who reiterated a strong currency alliance with the US and concern over the yen's underlying moves. However, broader Asian currencies weakened as the dollar held near a two-month high.


GEOPOLITICAL RISK

Geopolitical tensions remain elevated, contributing to market uncertainty and driving oil prices higher due to "rekindled war fears." The US and China are moving to lower tariffs on $60 billion of goods, a list that includes US imports of toys and Christmas decorations, and Chinese imports of US farm products, though soybeans were excluded. This development signals a potential de-escalation in trade tensions.


MARKET OUTLOOK

Traders will closely monitor the upcoming US jobs report for its impact on Treasury yields and broader market sentiment. Geopolitical developments, particularly regarding oil-producing regions and trade relations, will remain critical drivers. Technical levels for crude oil and natural gas will be key, alongside Micron's earnings report for insights into equity market growth drivers.
