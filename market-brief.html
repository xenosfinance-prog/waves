High-sulphur fuel oil prices in Asia are spiking above crude benchmarks due to Middle East and Russian trade dislocations.

Regulatory pressure intensifies as the SEC designates tokenization as a high-risk area for its 2027 market examination priorities. FOREX & RATES

The US Dollar index is stabilizing while foreign exchange markets navigate localized volatility and safe-haven flows. AI concerns and tech sector softness are driving capital into the Japanese Yen, placing downside pressure on the Euro. Short-term and long-term US inflation expectations are hovering at elevated levels, providing underlying support to Treasury yields. FX traders are closely watching trade policy shifts, including potential US tariff relief on select Canadian forestry and agricultural products.


GEOPOLITICAL RISK

Tensions in the Middle East continue to escalate, marked by Saudi-Houthi friction and US military evacuations in the region. Geopolitical risk extends into space as US officials accuse Russia of reckless maneuvers near a Finnish intelligence satellite aiding Ukraine. Meanwhile, the US administration has issued a six-month waiver allowing Russian diesel sales to mitigate global fuel shortages before upcoming elections. Political uncertainty remains high as tight presidential races unfold in both Brazil and Israel.


MARKET OUTLOOK

Traders should focus on crude oil price action around the $120 Brent barrier as supply disruptions test physical delivery capabilities. Key risk catalysts over the next 24 to 48 hours include the upcoming Federal Reserve policy meeting and potential official announcements regarding US-Canada tariff reductions. Technical support for Bitcoin rests near $80,000, with further downside risk toward $78,000 if global risk aversion deepens. Equity markets will monitor incoming earnings updates to gauge how persistent commodity inflation impacts corporate margins.
