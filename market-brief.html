MARKET OUTLOOK

Market direction over the next 24 to 48 hours centers on the release of US PCE inflation figures, which will validate or challenge the 78% probability of a December Fed hike. Traders monitor whether Brent can sustain critical support at $109 and if Bitcoin can stabilize above the $80,000 threshold.
