MACRO & CENTRAL BANKS

US CPI inflation for October topped forecasts, prompting concerns for investors and raising odds of further Fed tightening. Fed Governor Waller indicated he is leaning towards a December rate hike and could support a half-point increase depending on data. This hawkish sentiment led to a spike in US Treasury yields, with the 10-year reclaiming 5% and a $42 billion auction clearing at 5.02%. Markets are pricing a 96% probability of a Fed rate hike in December.


EQUITIES

US stocks are falling in response to the 10-year Treasury yield reclaims 5% and rising rate hike expectations. Bitcoin and US stocks initially declined after the inflation beat, reflecting broad market pressure. Key individual stock movers include Caterpillar, Intel, and AMD, while Anthropic targets a $1.4 trillion valuation in a forthcoming IPO. Equities broadly face headwinds from the prospects of continued monetary tightening.


COMMODITIES & ENERGY

Oil prices are declining to their lowest levels since August, with Brent crude briefly falling below $90 per barrel. This selloff is linked to hopes of diplomatic breakthroughs in Iran and Ukraine, coupled with signals of de-escalation in the Middle East. US gasoline prices have dipped below $4 per gallon for the first time since July amid the crude price drop. However, China's crude oil imports reached their highest since March, and ADNOC Gas reported a strong profit recovery, offering some counterpoints.


CRYPTO

Bitcoin is nearing an 11-week low after the "discouraging" US CPI inflation beat, as traders price in further Fed rate increases. The cryptocurrency market broadly experiences pressure from macro headwinds and tightening monetary policy expectations. Binance issued a warning regarding an iOS flaw that could expose crypto wallet keys for iPhone users. Separately, Mercado Bitcoin plans to resume cash withdrawals following a $49 million hack, and Coinbase joined a lawsuit backing the CFTC over US stock perp futures.


FOREX & RATES

US Treasury yields spiked as the hotter CPI data combined with resilient labor market reports. The US Treasury sold $42 billion of 10-year notes at a high yield of 5.02%, reflecting increased investor demands. The US dollar steadies on these aggressive Fed tightening bets, causing the British pound to slip. Northwest European diesel margins dropped over 7%, while the FDA warned of a possible vision loss risk for GLP-1 drugs, impacting related market segments.


GEOPOLITICAL RISK

Ukraine peace talks are intensifying, with the first US-Ukraine-Russia talks since February scheduled, potentially easing some geopolitical tensions impacting energy markets. In the Middle East, the Israeli president warns against repeat elections during a new Knesset session amid political gridlock. The US midterm shake-up could also reshape global oil and gas markets, though the implications for US policy are still unfolding.


MARKET OUTLOOK

Traders will continue to closely monitor any further rhetoric from Federal Reserve officials regarding the path of interest rates, particularly ahead of the December FOMC meeting. The primary focus remains on inflation trends and the sustained impact of higher Treasury yields across all asset classes. Commodity markets will watch for geopolitical developments that could influence oil supply, while crypto assets remain sensitive to broader macro cues.
