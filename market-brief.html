MARKET OUTLOOK

Traders should closely monitor upcoming inflation data and central bank commentary for further clues on monetary policy direction. Geopolitical developments in Ukraine and the Middle East will continue to influence commodity prices and broader market sentiment in the next 24-48 hours.
