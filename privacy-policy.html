Privacy Policy
Last updated: August 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data XenosFinance collects when you use the platform, how it is used, and the choices available to you. XenosFinance is operated by a single independent developer and does not sell personal data to third parties.

1. Who We Are

XenosFinance (xenosfinance.com) is an independently operated financial intelligence platform providing market analysis, Elliott Wave AI tools, trading signals, and related content.

2. Information We Collect

CategoryExamples
Usage dataPages visited, language selected, general session activity (via standard browser/server logs)
Device dataBrowser type, approximate location derived from IP, device type
Account dataPremium access codes you enter, wallet address if you make a payment on the Base network
CommunicationsYour Telegram username/ID if you interact with the XenosFinance Telegram bot; messages you send us directly
Local preferencesLanguage selection and similar settings stored in your browser

We do not require account registration with an email or password to access free content, and we do not knowingly collect sensitive personal data such as government ID numbers or financial account details.

3. How We Use Information

  • To operate and improve the platform (dashboards, calendar, signals, brief, blog);
  • To verify premium membership status and access codes;
  • To deliver content through the Telegram bot to users who choose to interact with it;
  • To understand aggregate usage patterns and fix technical issues;
  • To translate content into your selected language.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

XenosFinance uses cookies and browser local storage for essential functionality, such as remembering your language preference and, where applicable, premium access status. We do not use these mechanisms for cross-site advertising tracking.

5. Third-Party Services

XenosFinance relies on the following categories of third-party infrastructure to operate:

  • Hosting & content delivery — the website is served via Cloudflare;
  • Translation — an automated translation service is used to render content in supported languages;
  • Payments — premium membership payments are processed on the Base blockchain network (USDC); XenosFinance can see the paying wallet address but does not collect your name, bank details, or card information;
  • Messaging — Telegram, if you choose to interact with the XenosFinance Telegram bot;
  • News aggregation — third-party financial news sources (e.g. CNBC, Yahoo Finance) are displayed for informational purposes; visiting linked articles is subject to that publisher's own privacy policy.

Each of these providers processes data under its own privacy policy, which we encourage you to review.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes. Data may be disclosed where required by law, to protect the platform against fraud or abuse, or to the service providers listed above solely as needed to operate the platform.

7. Data Retention

We retain usage and log data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, or as required by applicable law.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including under the EU General Data Protection Regulation, if applicable to you), you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data we hold about you, and to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us through the channels listed on the platform.

9. Children

XenosFinance is not directed at, and does not knowingly collect data from, individuals under the age of 18.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the platform after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

11. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent through the contact channels listed on the XenosFinance platform.
