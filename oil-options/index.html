How to read it

OVX is the 30-day implied volatility of oil options: the higher it is, the bigger the move the market is paying to protect against. Compared with realised volatility it shows whether options are expensive (event risk priced) or cheap. Skew compares out-of-the-money puts and calls: puts richer means fear of a drop, calls richer means fear of a supply spike. Open-interest walls are strikes where many contracts are open — levels that often act as magnets or barriers, above all close to expiry.
