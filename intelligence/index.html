How the desk works

Geopolitics, Macro and Markets desks. The desk follows the Middle East, the Russia–Ukraine war, China–Taiwan, North Korea, coups and states of emergency, and new tariffs or export controls; rate decisions of the Fed, ECB, Bank of Japan and Bank of England; surprises in US jobs, inflation and growth data; and sharp equity sell-offs or banking stress. Each event is shown with the markets it moves.

Energy desk. The desk watches 25 key oil facilities — Gulf export terminals, Russian ports and refineries, Libya, Nigeria, Turkey, India and the US Gulf Coast — through NASA FIRMS satellite fire data, compared with each site's own recent baseline so that routine flaring is ignored. It also tracks the Strait of Hormuz, the Red Sea, Suez, the Turkish Straits, Russian oil ports and Libya through global news event data, counting how many independent outlets report the same disruption, and follows OPEC+ output decisions, new sanctions on Russian, Iranian and Venezuelan oil, and strikes on Russian energy infrastructure. A headline only counts when it reports a decision or event that has happened, not speculation.

Reliability: High two independent kinds of source agree (satellite and news) or many independent outlets report the same event. Medium one kind of source only, reviewed by the desk before publication. Single-source reports are never published.
